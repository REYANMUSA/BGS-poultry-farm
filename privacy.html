Privacy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

DealTruth is a buyer-assistance tool. When you create an account, we process the email address and authentication data needed to operate your account. If you save checks, DealTruth stores the product URL and the report fields needed to show your saved history, such as product name and seller.

Product links are sent to our server-side analyzer so it can request the public product page and extract available buyer signals. We do not ask for payment-card information and DealTruth does not process purchases.

Saved account data is stored with Supabase. Your browser also communicates with GitHub Pages to load the website. GitHub states that GitHub Pages visitors' IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes.

Deal reports are snapshots. Prices, stock, shipping, sellers, ratings and reviews can change. Missing information is not proof that a product is safe or a good deal.

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact the project owner through the repository's public contact channels.
